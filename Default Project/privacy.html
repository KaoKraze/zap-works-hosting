Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Zap Works ("we", "us") collects through zap-works.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Zap Works is a Roblox game studio run from the United States (Massachusetts) and Australia. Questions about privacy go to zapworks@outlook.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you use the contact or partner form: your name, email address, subject, message, the page you sent it from, and your confirmation that you're 13 or older. If you email us, we receive whatever you include in the email.
  • Basic technical data. To stop spam, our server briefly keeps your IP address in memory to limit how many messages can be sent; it is not saved and is cleared within about 10 minutes. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

The game and group statistics on this site come from Roblox's public APIs. They describe games and communities, not you.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No cookies. Your browser's session storage is used only to cache public game statistics so pages load faster; it clears when you close the tab.
  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

How we use it

To read and reply to your message, to evaluate partnership, acquisition and job inquiries, and to keep the site secure and free of spam.

Services that handle it for us

  • Discord — form messages are delivered to a private channel on our Discord server. Discord Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Outlook — our email. Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Railway — hosts this website. Railway Privacy Policy
  • Roblox — game images on this site load from Roblox's servers, so Roblox receives your IP address when your browser fetches them. Roblox Privacy Policy

Our team works from the United States and Australia, and these providers may process data in other countries. We only share what's needed to run the site and answer you.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to handle your request and any work that follows from it, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your message at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us by emailing zapworks@outlook.com. Depending on where you live — for example the EU or UK, Australia, or certain US states — you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to your local privacy regulator (in Australia, the OAIC). We'll respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is for businesses, creators and job applicants, not for children. You must be 13 or older to use our forms. If you're under 18, please get a parent's or guardian's permission before contacting us. If we learn we've received information from a child under 13, we'll delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and only our team can see the private channel where messages arrive. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send passwords or other sensitive information through the form.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
